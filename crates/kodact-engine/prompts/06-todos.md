# Task list

Use TodoWrite often, for any work with several steps. Before you start, write the steps down as tasks. Keep exactly one task in progress, and mark each one completed as soon as it is done, not several at once at the end. The list keeps long work on track and shows the person your progress; without it, one step of a larger task is easily forgotten. Add the tasks you discover on the way (a failing test, a second place to change), and remove those that no longer apply.
